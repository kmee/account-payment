This module lets a payer split a card payment into installments, as is
usual in Brazil and in other countries.

It is the provider-independent part of the feature:

- installment plans, per provider and payment method, with the options
  offered (2x, 3x ... 12x), the interest of each option, who pays it and
  a minimum installment amount;
- an installment selector in the payment form (eCommerce checkout,
  customer portal, payment links);
- the number of installments, the interest and the amount charged,
  stored on the payment transaction.

Sending the number of installments to the gateway is the job of each
provider module.

Configuration
=============

The *Installments* tab is shown on the providers whose module supports
installments.

1. Open the provider and go to the *Installments* tab.
2. Create a plan: pick the payment methods it applies to (Card by
   default) and add one line per option, with the number of
   installments, the interest in percent of the amount and who pays it.
3. Optionally set a minimum installment amount.

|Installment plan|

The single installment is always offered and needs no line.

.. |Installment plan| image:: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OCA/account-payment/18.0/payment_installment/static/description/installment_plan.png

Usage
=====

On the payment form, the payer picks the payment method and then the
number of installments:

|Installment selector on the payment form|

The confirmation page shows how the payment was split:

|Payment confirmation|

The transaction keeps what was chosen:

|Payment transaction|

When the interest is paid by the customer, the payer is charged the
amount plus the interest (*Amount Charged*); the amount of the
transaction stays the amount of the document being paid. When it is paid
by the company, the option is shown as interest-free and the interest is
recorded on the transaction.

**For provider modules**

- set ``support_installments`` in ``_compute_feature_support_fields``;
- read ``installments`` on the transaction when building the payment
  request;
- write ``installments`` and ``amount_charged`` if the gateway reports
  other values.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The interest is not posted to the accounts.
- A saved card (token) is always charged in a single installment.
- The plan is the same for every card brand.
