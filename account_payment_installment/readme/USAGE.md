1. Open an installment plan and add the journals of the card payments.
2. On an invoice, click *Pay* and choose one of those journals: the wizard shows
   *Installments* and the card fields.
3. Leave *Installments* empty for a single installment.

An option whose interest is charged by the company cannot be chosen here: that
interest has to be on the invoice, so it is added before invoicing (see *Sale
Payment Installments*).
