1. Create the acquirer (*Accounting > Configuration > Card Acquirers*): the
   partner, the journal of the entries and the accounts (receivable, clearing,
   fees, and optionally anticipation, adjustments and chargebacks).
2. Add the rules of the contract. Installments financed by the issuer are
   usually paid at once (*Single Settlement*); installments financed by the
   company are paid one by one.
3. Choose which installment takes the cents of the division, and the tolerance
   accepted when the acquirer puts them elsewhere.
4. On the journals of the card payments, set the acquirer. Their inbound payment
   methods need an outstanding account: it is where the payment waits before
   being moved to the acquirer.
